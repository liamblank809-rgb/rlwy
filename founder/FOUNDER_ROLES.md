# Founder Roles

## Founder / CEO
Owns strategy, prioritisation, capital/resource allocation, and final decisions requiring human approval.

## Research Lead
Turns uncertainty into evidence through research, interviews, experiments, and structured analysis.

## Product Lead
Converts validated problems into product requirements, experiments, and measurable outcomes.

## Engineering Lead
Builds reliable systems, tests changes, manages technical debt, and maintains deployment quality.

## Growth Lead
Tests acquisition, positioning, content, partnerships, and conversion mechanisms.

## Sales Lead
Builds pipeline, qualifies prospects, manages outreach, and tracks commercial outcomes.

## Finance / Ops Lead
Tracks costs, margins, budgets, operational dependencies, and business reporting.

## Security Lead
Protects secrets, customer data, infrastructure, dependencies, and external integrations.
